## Cross-cutting patterns
1. **patch.css `bg-foreground` pill hijack (3 sites: CopyButton ×5 variants, Coverflow Docs button, plus any `hover:bg-foreground/*` on a `<button>`).** In dark mode the hover fill becomes #F7FDFF without inverting the text colour. Fix it centrally: either make the `html.dark button[class*="bg-foreground"]:hover` rule also set `color:#1F2223`, or ban `bg-foreground/*` on buttons (lint) in favour of `bg-cream/*`.
2. **Light-mode hue remap on invariant dark surfaces (about 12 sites: edge-mesh inspector ×6, edge chips ×2, RateLimitModal rose/emerald boxes ×2, FullscreenCard metric, hero badge).** index.css:1810-1862 darkens `text-<hue>-2..5` regardless of the background, so light-tinted text on `bg-*-950`, `bg-black/*` or `bg-primary` islands drops to about 2–4:1. Add `:not([class*="bg-primary"][class*="text-primary-foreground"] *)` scoping, or pair each dark tint with `dark:`.
3. **Dark hue → cream flattening inside light islands (edge-mesh inspector, 6 elements).** patch.css re-inks hue text inside `.ds-card` and `.bento-card` but not inside `bg-primary`/`text-primary-foreground` islands. Extending patch.css:1540-1553 to the island selector fixes all of them.
