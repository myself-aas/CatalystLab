## Cross-cutting patterns
- **Light-mode pastel hue text (`text-{emerald,cyan,amber,rose,purple}-{300,400}`) on light surfaces**, written for a dark canvas: **about 22 live** instances (PaymentCheckoutModal ×5, TrialActivationModal ×6, GetInTouchEmailModal ×2, SyncStatusBadge ×4, NewsletterModal ×1, TrialBanner and others) plus about 20 latent (BenchmarkCard:65, IntegrationChip:50, StatCard:59/61, WorkflowStepCard:65, PricingPlanCard:96, TelemetrySwatchCard ×6, LiveTelemetryPanel ×6, PresetChip, MarkdownRenderer). All measure about 1.4:1 to 2.6:1 on white. Dark mode is "fixed" only by accident, through patch.css's `text-<hue>-` remap. Fix centrally with a `status-*` token set (light -700 / dark cream) and a codemod.
- **patch.css substring collisions:** `[class*="bg-muted"]` catching `hover:bg-muted` (RoleContentGate ×2, plus 4 Low hover-loss cases); `[class*="absolute|fixed inset-0"][class*="bg-background"]` turning intentional veils into the charcoal scrim (RoleContentGate:73, ParallaxSection:60, GetInTouch:141); `button[class*="bg-foreground"]` (BlogCard:141, PillCTA glass/full-width, PaymentCheckout:293, CatalystCarouselRail disabled buttons :103/:117); `text-<hue>-` remap inside invariant-white containers (SyncStatusBadge in the footer, WorkflowStepCard:77). That's about 13 instances. Add a lint rule banning `hover:bg-muted`, `hover:bg-foreground*` and `bg-background/<n>` on overlays, or narrow the patch selectors to exact classes.
- **Ink-on-scrim mismatch:** components on the always-dark `CardMedia` scrims use theme-flipping `text-foreground` / `text-muted-foreground` (LiveTelemetryPanel, CardByline in TestimonialCard), or white text sits on `from-background` gradients that turn white in light (EngineVectorCard). 3 latent instances. CardStatRow, CardTitle and CardSub already branch on `context.variant`; CardByline and the inline markup should do the same.
- **Modal infrastructure is duplicated 4 times** (Payment, Trial, GetInTouch, Newsletter), each without a focus trap and/or dialog semantics and with uncleared timers. Replace them with one Dialog primitive and lazy-load them from App.tsx.
- **Selection state not exposed** (`aria-pressed`, `aria-selected`): 9 live components, about 14 button groups.
- **Dead card library:** about 30 of the 44 `cards/` files (all of `marketing/` and most of `content/`, BlogCard, PricingPlanCard, TrailCard and its atomic parts, PillCTA, FavoriteButton, CardMedia consumers, the carousel hooks) plus `ParallaxCard`, `MarkdownRenderer` and `ExportReportDropdown` have no route consumers. Because `primitives/Card.tsx` statically attaches every sub-primitive (CardMedia, PillCTA, motion), the single live use in ui/BenchmarkCard pulls the whole compound into its chunk. Either delete the unused files or fix the latent issues above before adopting them.
