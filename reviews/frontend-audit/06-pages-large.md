## Cross-cutting patterns
- **Tailwind -400/-300 hue text on light surfaces (about 25 sites):** `text-emerald-400`, `text-amber-400`, `text-amber-300`, `text-rose-400`, `text-cyan-400` and `text-purple-400` on `bg-cream/10`, `bg-white/10`, `bg-background` or `ds-card`. All were written for the old dark-first UI and fail at 1.4 to 2.6:1 on the white canvas. The `text-success`, `text-warning` and `text-danger` tokens (already used in UserDashboardPage and MasterAuditExecutionPage) fix this centrally. A lint rule banning `text-(emerald|amber|rose|cyan|purple|sky)-[34]00` outside `dark:` would stop new cases.
- **Invariant dark surfaces with flipping text (3 sites):** the AdminDashboard header `bg-[rgba(31,34,35,.8)]`, the ProductsPage code island `bg-primary` without `text-primary-foreground`, and the EnginePlayground terminals that should be `ink-console`.
- **Unassociated `<label>`s and unnamed inputs (about 15 controls):** in PlaygroundPage and ProductsPage.
- **Toggle and tab groups without `aria-pressed`/`aria-selected` (about 12 groups):** across 5 files. A shared `SegmentedControl` / `Tabs` primitive would fix them all.
- **Whitespace-stripped template strings (about 8 sites):** curl, Go, YAML and JSX text, plus `console`→`logger` replacements inside user-facing snippets (3 sites). A codemod damaged string literals, so snippet generators need a shared tested module.
- **Garbled Tailwind class strings (about 12 sites):** `" py-0.5"`, `".5 py-1"` and `gap-1px-2.5`, which look like a broken `px-*` find-and-replace.
- **Fabricated or placeholder numbers (3 sites):** a saved `score: 92` (ToolPage, twice), the `?? 95` display fallback (Playground) and the About team roster, all contrary to the no-fabrication decision.
- **Async effects and handlers without cancellation or AbortController (7 sites):** in Playground, EnginePlayground, UserDashboard, DomainReport, MasterAudit and ToolPage.
- **API-key handling (2 pages):** each takes a different and broken approach (prefix sent as the key, or the secret displayed). It needs one design: session or proxy auth, plus placeholder snippets.
