## Cross-cutting patterns
- **patch.css `bg-foreground` pill collision (13 sites in 5 files).** In Navbar, MainMenuOverlay, DashboardShell, NavbarSearch and FinalCTA, every `hover:bg-foreground/*` and `bg-foreground/10` on an `a` or `button` turns into a pill CTA, and in dark it turns white-on-white on hover. Two ways to fix it centrally: tighten patch.css:1052-1066 to `[class~="bg-foreground"]` (exact token), or sweep these to `cream/*`.
- **Light-mode 400-shade hues (about 35 sites).** `text-emerald/amber/rose/purple/cyan-300/400` were written for dark. patch.css remaps only the blue family in dark and nothing in light. The worst areas are StickyHUD, the auth gates, the Navbar dropdown, MainMenuOverlay and the home cards. Fix with a lint rule, or a light-mode remap in patch.css next to the neutral remaps (l.1208).
- **`.ds-card` white-in-dark island (F2) misses `bg-surface` and `text-info` (3 sites).** Extend patch.css:1504-1513 rather than fixing each component.
- **`[role="dialog"]` / `[role="menu"]` patch.css rules hit wrapper elements (4 sites).** Put roles on the visual panel, or scope the patch to `:not(.fixed)`.
- **Focus visibility.** patch.css `input:focus{outline:none!important}` removes focus indication from every input that has no ring of its own: the hero URL field, the ROI sliders, and probably more outside this slice. Replace it with `:focus:not(:focus-visible)`.
- **Two full Firestore `blogs` reads** on the homepage (NavbarSearch on every route, plus LatestNewsAndInsights per tab). Cache `getBlogPosts` behind one module-level promise.
- **No global `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.** JS motion (`whileInView`, infinite loops, hover scale) ignores the user's preference. The CSS reduced-motion block only covers keyframe classes.
- **Out of checklist, but worth raising.** Testimonials.tsx:322-347 shows invented people (pravatar.cc faces, invented names and companies) with a `BadgeCheck` "verified" icon. These are fabricated reviews presented as real, which is a trust and FTC risk. MainMenuOverlay and DashboardShell show fake workspace and plan data.
