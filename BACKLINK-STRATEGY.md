## 2. Anchor Text Distribution Strategy
To maintain a natural, penalty-immune anchor text profile:
- **Brand Anchors (50-60%)**: "CatalystLab", "CatalystLab.tech", "CatalystLab telemetry", "catalystlab".
- **Partial-Match / LSI (20-25%)**: "CatalystLab web health analyzer", "CatalystLab edge latency radar", "CatalystLab OWASP compliance tool".
- **Naked URLs (10-15%)**: `https://www.catalystlab.tech/`, `www.catalystlab.tech`.
- **Generic Anchors (<5%)**: "visit platform", "learn more", "view dossier".
- **Exact-Match Keyword Anchors (<5%)**: "website health analyzer", "OWASP security header scanner", "AI readiness audit".

## 3. Toxic Link & Risk Prevention Protocols
### High-Risk Indicators (Zero Tolerance / Immediate Disavow)
- Links from Private Blog Networks (PBNs) or automated link farm networks (>10K outbound links/page).
- Unnatural exact-match anchor spikes from newly registered deindexed domains.
- Scraped low-quality directory blast submissions.

### Medium-Risk Monitoring
- Automated scraper sites republishing `/reports/:slug` dossiers without attribution.
- Reciprocal link rings across unrelated commercial verticals.
