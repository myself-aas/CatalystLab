Privacy Policy

Transparency is fundamental to our diagnostic telemetry. Learn how CatalystLab processes diagnostic requests, safeguards telemetry data, and protects user privacy.

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

CatalystLab ("we", "our", or "the Platform") operates an automated, non-invasive website diagnostic and web health benchmarking engine. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data integrity, confidentiality, and user privacy.

🔒 Privacy By Design
CatalystLab is built around passive diagnostic inspection. We analyze publicly accessible web pages without executing unauthorized code, injecting surveillance cookies, or extracting private credentials.

2. Telemetry & Information We Collect

When you interact with the CatalystLab platform, we collect certain technical data necessary to generate accurate 10-dimension health audits:

  • Target URLs Submitted: The public website address submitted for diagnostic evaluation.
  • Public DOM & HTTP Headers: Publicly reachable HTML markup, HTTP response status, SSL certificate details, and security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options).
  • Audit Telemetry: Computed Lighthouse Core Web Vitals, performance timings, carbon footprint estimates, and WCAG accessibility markers.
  • Diagnostic Email Deliveries: Email addresses provided voluntarily when requesting a dispatched PDF or HTML audit summary dossier.
  • Operational Logs: Request timestamps, IP addresses for volumetric abuse prevention (50 scans/day/IP), and standard user-agent strings.

3. How Information is Used

All gathered data is strictly utilized to deliver the core service:

  • Generating comprehensive 10-dimension radar scores, issue breakdowns, and actionable remediation guidelines.
  • Providing instant versioned comparisons (e.g., comparing domain performance over time or between competitors).
  • Dispatching requested audit summaries to your designated email inbox.
  • Enforcing server-side rate limits and safeguarding the diagnostic infrastructure against denial-of-service attempts.
🚫

Zero Advertising Trackers

We never sell, lease, or monetize user audit data or browsing activity to ad brokers.

🛡️

End-to-End Encryption

All API traffic, reports, and streaming SSE telemetry are transmitted strictly over TLS 1.3.

⚡

Ephemeral In-Memory Cache

Audit snapshots are cached for 6 hours to reduce redundant upstream load, then purged.

4. Local Storage & Cookie Utilization

CatalystLab does not use tracking or profiling cookies. We utilize standard client-side browser storage exclusively for user convenience:

  • localStorage: Stores your recent local audit history (up to 12 items) so you can revisit past audits directly from your device. You can clear this anytime with one click in the UI.
  • sessionStorage: Holds the active report data during page transitions between the Analyzer and Report views.

For further details, consult our dedicated Cookie & Local Storage Policy.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

CatalystLab does not sell personal data. We interact with minimal, trusted infrastructure providers strictly to execute service functionality:

  • Hosting & Serverless Compute: Secure cloud compute nodes hosting our diagnostic Node.js server.
  • Diagnostic Bots: Headless Chromium engines strictly performing non-destructive DOM and Core Web Vitals measurements.
  • Public Report Directory: Audits generated through the platform receive a unique versioned identifier and may be visible in the public directory unless marked private.

6. GDPR (EU) & CCPA (California) User Rights

Regardless of your geographical location, CatalystLab respects your fundamental digital rights:

  • Right of Access: You have the right to request a full copy of telemetry records associated with your domain or email.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request the deletion or delisting of any public report generated for a domain you own.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request that your domain be added to our exclusion blocklist to prevent automated scans.
  • Non-Discrimination: Exercising your privacy rights will never result in degraded service quality or penalty.

7. Data Retention & Erasure Policies

In-memory diagnostic caches expire automatically after 6 hours. Operational telemetry logs and rate limit IP hash tables are rotated every 24 hours. Email dispatch records older than 30 days are automatically purged.
