1. Our Zero Tracking Cookie Pledge

CatalystLab is built on ethical web principles. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, behavioural profiling tags, or cross-site tracking pixels (such as Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics Remarketing, or data broker beacons).

shield Clean, Tracker-Free Architecture
Because we do not store tracking cookies or sell behavioural profiles, our platform does not require intrusive, multi-layered cookie consent popups.

2. Browser Storage Utilization

We utilize standard, secure browser client-side storage technologies for purely functional user experience enhancements:

Storage Key Type Duration Purpose
catalystRecentAudits localStorage Persistent Saves up to 12 recent audits on your device for quick 1-click re-access.
catalystReport sessionStorage Tab Session Maintains active audit report state during page transitions.
catalystEmailNote sessionStorage Tab Session Temporarily preserves audit dispatch notes when sharing reports.

3. How to Clear or Manage Local Storage

You maintain full sovereignty over your browser storage. You can purge all cached audit history directly from the CatalystLab interface:

  • In the UI: Click the "Clear All History" button located on the homepage under the Recent Audits section.
  • In Your Browser: Open Developer Tools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → Clear catalystlab.tech data.

4. How We Handle Target Website Cookies

When our diagnostic bot scans a target URL, it inspects whether the target website sets secure cookie flags:

  • Secure: Ensures cookies are transmitted only via encrypted HTTPS.
  • HttpOnly: Prevents client-side scripts from reading sensitive authentication tokens (mitigating XSS).
  • SameSite: Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) exploits.

Our audit bots never persist, store, or forward any session cookies received from analyzed websites.

5. Compliance with ePrivacy & GDPR

CatalystLab operates strictly within the guidelines set out by the European Union ePrivacy Directive (Article 5(3)) and GDPR (Recital 30). Because client-side storage is strictly necessary for service operation and does not track personal identity, no additional tracking consent is required.

Questions regarding our storage policy? Reach our team at Contact & Support.
